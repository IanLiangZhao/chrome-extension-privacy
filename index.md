# Privacy Policy for Williams

Last updated: 16 November 2026

## What the extension does
Williams adds search, CSV export and refresh functions to
tables on au11.dotwms.com.

## Data collection
The extension does not collect, store or transmit any personal
information. It reads table data on the page only to provide its
features, and all processing happens locally in your browser.

## Data sharing
No data is sent to the developer or to any third party. CSV files
are generated locally and saved only to your own device.

## Permissions
The extension only runs on au11.dotwms.com and uses its permissions solely
to provide the features described above.
